KWONIL ONE V2

KWONIL ONE V2 is a personal AI workspace designed to help its user organize information, communicate, plan activities, and work with connected digital services through a unified conversational interface.

Google account integration

KWONIL ONE V2 can connect to a user's Google account only after the user explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth.

Authorized Google services may be used for features such as:

  • Viewing and working with Gmail messages
  • Accessing files in Google Drive
  • Viewing and managing Google Calendar information

Google account access is used only to provide features requested by the authorized user. KWONIL ONE V2 does not sell Google user data.

Personal-use project

KWONIL ONE V2 is currently operated as a personal-use AI system. Access to connected services is limited to authorized accounts and is controlled by the user.
